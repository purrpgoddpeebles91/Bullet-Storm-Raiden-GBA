# Roadmap

Target: a complete roguelite shoot-'em-up for Game Boy Advance, submitted to **GitHub Game Off 2026** (theme: **SHIFT**) before Dec 1, 2026, 13:37 PST, and expanded after the jam.

Legend: [x] done, [~] in progress, [ ] planned

## Design pillars
- Dream War story: a kid's daydream gone wrong; serious nightmare stages, silly dream stages. The world SHIFTS between them.
- Darius-style forks after every level and sub-level; every run plays differently.
- 7-slot power meter: 1 slot you choose, 6 rolled every run.
- Memory fragments unlock lore across runs; 12+ endings.
